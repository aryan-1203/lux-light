This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you visit our website or join early access. We have tried to write it in plain language. It is drafted with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") in mind.

## Who we are

This website is run by [Company legal name], a company registered in India with its registered office at [registered address] ("we", "us", "our").

For the purposes of the DPDP Act, we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here.

**Grievance officer:** [name, email]

## What we collect

### When you fill in the early-access form

- Your name
- Your phone number
- Your email address (if you give it)
- Your city and state
- Your interests and how you expect to use the lamp
- How many lamps you might want, and your budget range
- How you heard about us
- Whether you agree to be contacted on WhatsApp

### Collected automatically with your form

- Campaign details from the link you used (UTM parameters)
- The page that referred you to us, and the page you first landed on
- Your device's user-agent (a short text string that describes your browser and device type)

We do not ask for payment details, government ID numbers or health information. Please do not include health information in any free-text field.

## Why we collect it

We use your data to:

- Contact you about early access and the launch of the lamp
- Message you on WhatsApp, but only if you have opted in
- Understand which cities, uses and price ranges matter most, so we can plan the founding batch
- Understand which channels bring people to the site, in aggregate
- Keep the form free of spam and abuse

We will not use your data for any unrelated purpose without asking you first.

## Our lawful basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your **consent**, which you give when you submit the early-access form. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see below). Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did lawfully before you withdrew.

## Where your data is stored

Early-access responses are stored in Google Sheets, which runs on Google's cloud infrastructure. Google may store or process this data on servers outside India. Where data is transferred outside India, we will follow any restrictions notified by the Government of India under the DPDP Act.

Access within our team is limited to the people who need it to contact you or plan the launch.

## How long we keep it

We keep your early-access data until 12 months after the lamp launches, or until you withdraw your consent, whichever comes first. After that, we delete it or make it fully anonymous.

If you become a customer, any data needed for your order will be handled under a separate policy at that time.

## Who we share it with

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers.

We share data only with service providers who help us run the site and early access, and only as much as they need:

- **Google**, for storing form responses
- **Cloudflare Turnstile**, for spam protection on the form
- **Our website hosting providers**, to serve the site and process the form
- **WhatsApp (Meta)**, when you message us or we message you there after you opt in

We may also disclose data where required by Indian law, or to protect our legal rights.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it
- **Correct** or update data that is inaccurate or incomplete
- **Erase** your data, where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it
- **Grievance redressal**, by contacting our grievance officer, and then the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity

We aim to respond to requests within a reasonable time, and within any period set by law.

## How to withdraw consent or make a request

You can do either of the following:

- Send us a message on WhatsApp saying you would like your details removed or corrected
- Email [privacy email] from the address or with the phone number you used on the form

We may ask you to confirm a few details so we know the request is genuinely yours.

## Children

Early access is for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

## Cookies and analytics

We aim to keep tracking to a minimum.

- **Essential cookies or storage** may be used to keep the site and form working, and for spam protection.
- **Analytics**, if enabled, will be privacy-friendly analytics that measure visits in aggregate. They will not be used to build advertising profiles.
- We do **not** use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls and secure connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected people and the authorities as required by law.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we prepare for launch or as the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we will tell you by WhatsApp or email where we can.
