This started with an ordinary feeling, one most of us on the team know well.

A work-from-home desk facing a wall. Winter arrives, then the fog, then weeks of grey. Mornings blur into each other. Chai helps. Getting outside helps more, when the air allows it. But on most days, the brightest thing in the room is a laptop.

Or a run of night shifts. Sleeping through bright afternoons, waking up in the dark, never quite in step with the family.

Neither of these stories is unusual. A great many people in India live some version of them. Long hours indoors. Commutes that start before sunrise. Night shifts for customers on the other side of the world. Monsoon months when the sky stays heavy for days.

## What we kept running into

Bright light lamps have been around for years in other countries. When we looked for one here, we found a few problems.

Most were imported and priced for other markets. Many made bold health promises we were not comfortable with. Few had guidance written for Indian homes, Indian weather or Indian work patterns. And after-sales support often meant an email address in another time zone.

## What we are trying to do

We want to make a well-built, UV-free, 10,000 lux lamp that is honest about what it does. It supports mood, energy and a regular daily rhythm. It is not a medical device, and we will never pretend it is.

We also want to explain things properly. When to use it, when not to, and who should speak to a doctor first. In plain language, with the chai and the fog and the night shifts included.

And we want to be reachable. On WhatsApp, in your time zone, by people who actually use the lamp.

## Starting carefully

We are starting small, with a founding batch, and testing as we go. Where a number still needs checking, we will say so. Where we do not know something, we will say that too.

If that sounds like the kind of company you would like to buy from, we would love to hear from you.
