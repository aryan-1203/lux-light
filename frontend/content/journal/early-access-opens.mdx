Early access is now open.

For the last few months we have been testing, adjusting and testing again. We are now ready to start talking to the people who will use the lamp first. If you would like to be one of them, you can join from our home page.

## What early access means

Early access is a simple way to tell us you are interested. You share a few details: your name, phone number, city, and a little about how you might use the lamp. That helps us plan the founding batch around real people rather than guesses.

In return, you will be first to hear when the lamp is ready, and you will be offered founding-batch pricing.

## What it does not mean

Signing up is not an order. We do not take any payment now, and you are under no obligation to buy. If you change your mind, you can ask us to remove your details at any time.

## What happens next

If you opt in to WhatsApp, we will send you a short message to confirm. After that, we will only get in touch when there is something useful to share:

- Updates on timelines as the founding batch comes together
- A chance to answer a few questions about how you would use the lamp
- Your founding-batch offer, when it is ready

We will not flood your phone. A few messages, each one worth reading.

If you prefer email, that works too. Just tell us on the form.

## A small ask

If someone you know spends long days indoors, works nights or dreads the winter fog, please share the page with them. Early access is open to anyone in India.

Thank you for being here this early. It means a lot.
