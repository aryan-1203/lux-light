We could have started big. Ordered a large first run, filled a warehouse and hoped for the best. We decided not to.

Our first batch will be small. We are calling it the founding batch, and the people who receive it will help us decide what the lamp becomes.

## Why small

A light lamp looks simple, but the details matter. How the stand feels on a cramped desk. Whether the buttons make sense in the half-dark at six in the morning. How the light feels at the far end of a dining table. How the packaging survives a courier ride in the monsoon.

We can test a lot of this ourselves, and we are. But we will learn far more from real homes than from any lab. A smaller batch means we can listen properly, and act on what we hear before we make more.

## How we are testing

Before the founding batch ships, every design goes through checks we care about:

- Light output and colour at the recommended distance, measured with a meter
- Timer, brightness levels and modes, used daily for weeks
- Heat, stability and build after long sessions
- Packaging, through real courier routes

Where a figure still needs confirming, we say so. Every number will be checked before launch.

## The feedback loop

If you are in the founding batch, we will ask you a few simple questions over WhatsApp after a week or two. What works. What annoys you. What you wish it did. You can reply in a sentence or a voice note, whatever is easier.

Everything we hear goes into the next batch. Better instructions, a tweak to the stand, a change to the timer. Small things that add up.

## Joining

The founding batch will come with founding-batch pricing. You can join through early access on our home page. No payment, no commitment, just a way to stay in touch.
